PFFFT — a Pretty Fast Fast Fourier Transform.

Used by LofiPod's UpcConvolver (FIR EQ modes: Min-Phase FIR, Linear-Phase
FIR, Mixed-Phase) for forward/inverse real FFTs and the canonical
packed-complex multiply-accumulate inner loop of partitioned convolution.

Source: https://github.com/marton78/pffft
        (Maintained fork of Julien Pommier's original PFFFT, with CMake
         support and additional contributions. Upstream:
         https://bitbucket.org/jpommier/pffft)

The PFFFT code reuses FFTPACK pieces; both are under the same
permissive BSD-3-Clause-style license reproduced below verbatim.
